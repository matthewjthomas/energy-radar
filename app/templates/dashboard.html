What is R²?

R² (R-squared) is a score from 0 to 1 that shows how much of your day-to-day usage change is explained by the weather. A value near 1 means usage mostly tracks temperature (heating and cooling). Near 0 means other factors dominate—appliances, schedules, occupancy, and so on. Energy Radar fits a simple model using heating and cooling degree-days, then reports how well that model matches your history.
